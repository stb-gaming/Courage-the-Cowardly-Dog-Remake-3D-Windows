What is this?
This is a 3D remake of three Courage the Cowardly Dog games, made by SeriousCsaba (2020-2024).
Original games by Denki:
Courage the Cowardly Dog - Katz Kidnap (2003)
Courage the Cowardly Dog - Katz Komeback (2004)
Courage the Cowardly Dog - Le Quack Attack (2004)

What are these Courage the Cowardly Dog games?
Sky Gamestar was a UK television game provider. It was a service in which Sky TV customers could play interactive games, using a TV remote as a controller. It was shut down on January 31st, 2015. Many games on the platform are now lost media, except these Courage the Cowardly Dog games which were released online by Denki.

System requirements
